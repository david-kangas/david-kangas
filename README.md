HapticLab

An experimental composer for designing and auditioning watchOS haptic sequences without repeatedly recompiling an app.

The interface treats haptics more like a timeline or mixing board, making it easier to explore timing, rhythm, sequencing, and the limited haptic vocabulary available on Apple Watch.

Built with: SwiftUI · watchOS · iOS

Rinse Club

A deliberately unserious 21+ social check-in app for shower beers.

The joke is light; the product thinking underneath it is not. Rinse Club is being designed around minimal data collection, broad regional rather than precise location information, self-service data deletion, and a low-friction Rinse / Repeat check-in model.

How I Build

I like products that:

* Solve one clear problem well
* Stay out of the user’s way
* Treat privacy and security as product decisions, not legal cleanup
* Use automation where it removes friction rather than adding complexity
* Favor durable systems over unnecessary complexity
* Feel human, useful, and occasionally a little ridiculous

I’m especially interested in the point where product design, operations, governance, and software engineering overlap.
